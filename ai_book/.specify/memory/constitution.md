# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics AI-Native Textbook Constitution

## Core Principles

### I. Purpose
To provide a comprehensive,research on AI-Native textbook on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics.

### II. Audience
Academics and students in computer science, robotics, and artificial intelligence, with an assumed foundational understanding of these fields.

### III. Tone
Objective, academic, rigorous, and forward-looking.

### IV. Level
Advanced undergraduate to graduate level.

### V. Structure Rules
Modular chapters, logical flow from foundational concepts to advanced applications, and consistent terminology.

### VI. Content Boundaries
Focus exclusively on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics, excluding general AI topics not directly relevant.

### VII. Code Style
Python with PEP 8 guidelines, clear comments, and reproducible examples.

### VIII. Learning Philosophy
Emphasis on hands-on application, critical thinking, and a deep understanding of underlying principles rather than rote memorization.

## Key Standards

- All factual claims must be traceable to sources
- Citation format: APA style
- Source types: minimum 50% peer-reviewed articles
- Plagiarism check: 0% tolerance before submission
- Writing clarity: Flesch-Kincaid grade 10-12

## Constraints

- Word count: 5,000-7,000 words
- Minimum 15 sources
- Format: PDF with embedded citations

## Success Criteria

- All claims verified against sources
- Zero plagiarism detected
- Passes fact-checking review

## Governance

This Constitution serves as the foundational governance document for the "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics AI-Native Textbook" project, superseding all other conflicting practices.
Amendments to this Constitution require a documented proposal, review by project stakeholders, and explicit approval.
Versioning follows semantic versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH) for clarity.
Compliance reviews will be conducted periodically to ensure adherence to these principles and standards.
